Support

Need help? Here are answers to the most common questions.

Apps aren't being blocked.

Open Focus Crew › Blocks and make sure Screen Time shows as connected and your focus block list isn't empty. Blocking also requires a device passcode.

How do I see my companion next to my Mac's notch?

Install Focus Crew for Mac, sign in to the same iCloud account on both devices, and keep Sync sessions to Mac turned on in the iPhone app's Settings.

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and search for Focus Crew. Lock Screen widgets are added from Customize on the Lock Screen.

How do I remove all blocks?

End any running session, switch off your schedules in Blocks, or use Settings › Reset all data. You can also revoke access in iOS Settings › Screen Time › Apps with Screen Time Access.

Still stuck?

Open an issue on GitHub and we'll get back to you.
