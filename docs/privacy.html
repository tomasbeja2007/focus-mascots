Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Focus Crew is built so that your data stays with you. We don't run servers that receive your data, we don't have accounts, and we don't use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

What stays on your device

Your onboarding answers, focus sessions, schedules, badges and settings are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app's private storage (shared with its own widgets and extensions through an App Group). Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also erase it at any time in Settings › Reset all data.

Screen Time data

Focus Crew uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity) to block the apps and websites you choose and to show your screen time in the Insights tab. Apple represents the apps you pick as opaque tokens: we cannot see which apps they are. Usage reports are rendered by an Apple-sandboxed extension that cannot send data anywhere. None of this information ever leaves your device or reaches us.

iCloud

If you use the Mac companion, the app writes a small status summary (your first name, chosen companion, the running session's timer and today's focus total) to your own iCloud key-value storage so your Mac can show it. This lives in your iCloud account, governed by Apple's privacy policy, and is not accessible to us. You can turn it off in Settings › Sync sessions to Mac.

Notifications

Reminders and session alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push notification service is used.

Purchases

Focus Crew is currently free. If in-app purchases are offered in the future, they will be processed entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Children

Focus Crew does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Data we collect

None. The App Store privacy label for Focus Crew is "Data Not Collected".
