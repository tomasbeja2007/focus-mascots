What the app does

Focus Crew helps you limit distractions using Apple's Screen Time features. Blocking depends on iOS and can be changed by you at any time in the app or in iOS Settings. Focus Crew is a productivity aid, not a medical or parental-control product.

Strict mode

Strict mode makes it harder to end a session early and may prevent apps from being deleted while a session or strict schedule is active. You can always end a strict session by holding the give-up button.
